This report compares the licensing position of three YOLO-style object detection options for use in a project that may require commercial or proprietary deployment.

## License explanations

### AGPL-3.0

AGPL-3.0 is a strong copyleft open-source license. It allows use, study, modification, and redistribution, but if the software is used as part of a larger application, especially one made available over a network, the corresponding source code generally needs to be made available under the same license.

For this project, the important point is that Ultralytics treats use of its YOLO code, models, training pipeline, and fine-tuned models as subject to AGPL-3.0 unless an Enterprise License is obtained. Therefore, Ultralytics YOLO is suitable as a research or technical baseline, but it is not the safest default choice for a proprietary commercial product.

### Ultralytics Enterprise License

The Ultralytics Enterprise License is a paid commercial license. Its purpose is to allow companies and private projects to use Ultralytics YOLO without open-sourcing the entire application under AGPL-3.0.

For a closed-source product, internal business tool, SaaS system, embedded deployment, edge device, robotics application, or commercial fine-tuned model, this is the license route Ultralytics recommends.

**Ultralytics Enterprise Licese pricing.** Ultralytics does not publish a standard Enterprise License price. Pricing is custom and
depends on the organization, deployment scale, and use case. A quote must be requested
from Ultralytics. Public Ultralytics Platform subscription prices, such as the Pro plan,
are separate from the Enterprise License needed for proprietary commercial use of
Ultralytics YOLO.

### Apache-2.0

Apache-2.0 is a permissive open-source license. It generally allows commercial use, modification, redistribution, private use, and sublicensing. It also includes an express patent license from contributors.

The main obligations are usually to preserve copyright notices, include the license text, state significant modifications where required, and retain notices. Unlike AGPL-3.0, Apache-2.0 does not normally require the larger proprietary application to be open-sourced.

For this project, Apache-2.0 models such as YOLOX and PP-YOLOE are more suitable candidates for commercial-friendly comparison, assuming any pretrained weights, datasets, and third-party dependencies used in the final pipeline are also checked.

## Practical recommendation

Use Ultralytics YOLO11 as the high-quality technical baseline, but use YOLOX or PP-YOLOE as the main commercial-friendly comparator. If the final system needs to remain proprietary and avoid paid model licensing, YOLOX and PP-YOLOE are safer starting points than Ultralytics YOLO.
